  1. 1

    Paste a link

    Copy the link of a public Instagram post or profile and paste it into the collector.

  2. 2

    Review the frames

    We lay out what we find as numbered, uncropped frames. Likely post images come first. Thumbnails of the account’s other posts sit below the line, and profile pictures are left out. The sort is an estimate, so each frame shows what it rests on.

  3. 3

    Download what you marked

    Mark the frames you want and download them. Nothing is selected by default, and nothing downloads until you ask.

Before you start

Public pages only
Private accounts and removed posts won’t work, and we never ask for your Instagram login.
Profiles show a few posts
A profile link returns the handful of recent posts Instagram shows publicly, not the whole account.
Results can be incomplete
A carousel can come back short. We can’t always tell how many images a post holds, so we never call a set complete.
Images, not video
A Reel link may return a still image. Video isn’t supported.
Your link leaves this page
The post link goes to our server and to Jina Reader, a public page reader. Images pass through our server so their bytes can be checked. We don’t store them or keep a history. A short-lived cookie ties your results to this browser.
Save only what you may
Use Insta Luck for images you’re authorized to save.
