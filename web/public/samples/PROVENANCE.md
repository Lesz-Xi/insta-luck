# Sample photographs

The eight frames on the landing page. They illustrate the selection model and are labeled there as a sample.

- **Author:** the maker of Insta Luck. These are their own photographs, chosen for this page on 2026-11-05. All rights reserved: they aren't covered by anything that licenses this repository's code.
- **Changes:** resized to 360 and 720 px wide and re-encoded as WebP (quality 80), which drops EXIF and XMP data. Each file keeps only a standard sRGB colour profile. No cropping or retouching.
- **Sample labels:** frames 04 and 05 share a real date (October 12, 2022), so "Same date as frame 01" is true of them. Frame 02's date is June 26, 2024.
- **Not included:** the rest of the local collection, or its ledger.

The reference to an Instagram post is left out on purpose, so this repository doesn't point at the maker's account.

The Atget photographs these replaced were public domain and remain in this repository's history.
